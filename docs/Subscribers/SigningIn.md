# Signing In

The Yntraa Cloud portal is a secure web-based interface for accessing and managing your cloud resources. Sign in to authenticate your account and access the features and services available.

To **Sign In** to your Yntraa Cloud, follow these steps:
1. Navigate to [Yntraa Cloud portal](https://portal.yntraacloud.ai/). The following screen appears:
![Yntraa Sign In](img/yntraasignin.png)
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click the **Sign In** button.
