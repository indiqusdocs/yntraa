# Subscribing to a Service

By subscribing to a service, you can utilize additional cloud services designed to meet your infrastructure and operational needs. The platform enables seamless service management from a single location.

To select and request a service, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Miscellaneous > Other Services**. The following screen appears:
   ![Other Services](img/otherservices.png)
2. Select the required service and category from the **Service Name** and **Service Category** dropdown. The following screen appears: 
   ![Service Category](img/servicecategory.png)
3. Click **Select** button next to the required service and verify the selected service details in the **Summary** section. The following screen appears: 
   ![Select Services](img/selectservices.png)
4. Click the **Submit Request** button. The confirmation screen appears, where you can review the service request details:
   ![Confirm Service](img/confirmservice.png)
5. Click **Confirm** button to proceed.
   
The service order is generated in One Yotta, where you can view the order details.
